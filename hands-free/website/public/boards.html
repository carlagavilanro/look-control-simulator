HANDS FREE

Boards and Wi-Fi

Put the program on a board and tell it your Wi-Fi, from this page. Nothing to install on the computer. Plug the board into this computer with a USB cable that carries data, and use Chrome or Edge.

This browser cannot talk to a board over USB. Open this page in Chrome or Edge on a computer (not a phone or tablet).

1 Plug the board in

No board connectedPlug one board in, then press Connect and pick it in the list the browser shows.

In the browser's list the board is usually called "USB Serial", "CP210x" or "CH340". If the list is empty, the cable may be a charging-only cable: try another one.

2 The program

Which board is this?
StartingLeave the board plugged in. This takes about a minute.

Installing keeps what the board has saved: its Wi-Fi, its link to your devices and the receiver's recordings. If it cannot start, hold the button marked BOOT on the board, press Install again, and let go when the bar starts to move.

3 Wi-Fi

The name and password go from this page straight to the board, through the USB cable. They are not sent to this website and not kept in this browser. The board needs a 2.4 GHz network (most home Wi-Fi has one; a phone hotspot may need "Maximize compatibility" switched on). Capital letters matter.

4 Link it to your devices

Done: go to Setup →

Linking is done once for each board. Afterwards the board only needs power: unplug it from the computer and plug it into any USB charger.
